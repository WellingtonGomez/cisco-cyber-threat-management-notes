# Module 1.4 – Governance and Compliance Summary

## Governance
- Governance determines who makes cybersecurity decisions.
- Ensures risks are managed and security aligns with business goals.
- Key roles: Owner, Controller, Processor, Custodian, Steward, DPO.

## Ethics of Cybersecurity
- Follow laws and organizational policies.
- Respect privacy, data, and intellectual property.
- Three ethics approaches:
  - Utilitarian
  - Rights-Based
  - Common Good

## Cybercrime & Laws
### Cybercrime Types
- Computer-Targeted
- Computer-Assisted
- Computer-Incidental

### Important Laws
- FISMA
- CFAA
- ECPA
- FERPA
- HIPAA
- COPPA

## IT Security Frameworks
- ISO 27000
- NIST Workforce Framework
- CIS Controls
- Cloud Security Alliance (CSA)
- CMMC

## Key Takeaways
- Governance provides accountability.
- Compliance ensures legal and regulatory requirements are met.
- Ethics guide cybersecurity decisions.
- Security frameworks help organizations manage risk and improve security.
